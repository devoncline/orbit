# Changelog

## Unreleased

### Improvements

- Home and Learn now have distinct starting experiences: Home offers class, curiosity, and material paths; Learn opens a focused topic entry that starts studying directly. Existing spaces can be reused, and active sessions resume through Learn.
- Orbit now remembers learning spaces, syllabus context, mastery, learner corrections, and useful learning anchors in the same browser. Home continues from recent work, with explicit corrections taking priority over inferred starting points.
- Recent study threads retain compact summaries, the latest lesson, and two recent exchanges after refresh. A confirmed Library control clears local learning data. Memory stays on this device, with relevant context used by the existing learning service; there is no account or cross-device sync.
- Mobile navigation now opens from a compact menu icon immediately before the Orbit logo, with an accessible label and a comfortable touch target.
- Study now keeps one interaction in focus, with compact access to earlier exchanges and separate study threads. Switching threads preserves live drafts, activities, quizzes, and learning context.
- Recommendations and new lessons use today's date and syllabus timing to suggest a relevant starting point. Learners can correct that position, skip familiar material, or say a topic has not started; unclear timelines ask for a starting point, and past material remains available for review.
- Actions across Orbit have clearer surfaces and hover/focus feedback, with fewer decorative arrows and easier-to-tap Study controls on mobile.
- Learn keeps the shared desktop sidebar. Orbit stays fully visible on small screens, smiles between replies, and uses the same gentle floating motion everywhere, switching to a thinking pose while preparing a response.
- Home now leads with the current learning task or question and a contextual action. Study gives Orbit a larger visual stage, with a dedicated mobile composition, calmer motion, and clearer controls. Calendar, Library, and space setup use simpler copy and more open layouts with fewer dividers.
- Orbit now has learning spaces for classes, skills, and personal interests, with a calm shared navigation, a mobile menu, and three ways to begin from an empty account. Home keeps one next action in focus and uses Orbit’s existing environment and companion artwork.
- A lightweight Calendar and Home preview gather dated work from learning materials, keeping unclear dates separate for review. Materials can be added directly to a chosen space, with visible upload progress and retry feedback.
- Study now has a quieter environment, clear Learn/Practice progress, and a way to return Home and resume the current session.
- Orbit can now remember useful connections, successful activities, and tentative misconceptions during study, then reuse relevant examples in later teaching and practice. These memories do not change mastery.
- Learn now offers "Let me try it": a short, practical activity based on the current concept and conversation. Learners can share what they tried, get focused feedback, and keep talking or choose "Quiz me". Activities stay in the current session and do not change mastery.
- Learners can now ask questions or share connections during a lesson and receive short, contextual replies from Orbit. The lesson shortcuts and "Quiz me" remain available, with recent conversation kept compact.
- Study sessions now begin with a short, topic-aware lesson. Learners can ask for a different explanation or a concrete example, then choose "Quiz me" to practice the concept before continuing through adaptive questions.
- Home now places its learning recommendation and companion in a nebula environment, with dark text areas, soft edges, and a quieter crop on smaller screens.
- Orbit now uses its own browser and app icons. The Home companion has a more noticeable, calm floating motion while remaining static for reduced-motion users.
- Home now features the Orbit logo and companion artwork, with gentle floating motion that respects reduced-motion preferences and compact placement on smaller screens.
- Home now puts the next learning action first, with a calmer dark palette, a responsive hero, and easy-to-scan courses below. Course details, adding courses, and uploading materials remain available below the recommendation.
- Study question generation now asks Muse to choose a relevant topic, preferring weaker topics when they relate to upcoming work.
- Study answers now update mastery for the question's topic, with overall course mastery calculated as the rounded average of topic scores. Questions without a topic retain the existing course mastery behavior.

### Manual test checklist

- With no spaces, compare Home's three paths with Learn's single topic field. Enter a subject and press Enter or Start learning; confirm a lesson begins directly in a self-directed space. With one and seven spaces, choose an existing subject (including different capitalization) and verify progress is retained without duplicates. Leave Study for Home, then select Learn and confirm the active thread resumes. Check the entry form on mobile and with a keyboard.
- Start with zero, one, and seven spaces; refresh and reopen the browser. Check space names, syllabus information, upcoming work, course/topic mastery, and corrected starting points. Confirm Home offers a relevant continuation without an empty-state flash.
- Build a useful analogy or activity anchor, then refresh and resume a thread or create a related one. Check saved summaries, the lesson, and up to two recent exchanges; activities, quiz screens, drafts, and original uploads should not be restored. Only the opened thread should request a missing lesson.
- In Library, cancel clearing local data and verify nothing changes; confirm clearing, then refresh and verify a clean start. Try disabled/full browser storage and malformed saved data; Orbit should remain usable and warn when it cannot save.
- On a narrow screen, check the menu icon immediately before the logo, its touch target, hover/pressed/focus feedback, opening and closing, Escape, keyboard trapping in the drawer, and focus return to the opener.
- Create and switch between study threads, leave for Home, and resume. Verify drafts, conversation, activities, quiz feedback, and mastery remain intact. After several conversation turns on a phone-sized screen, confirm only the latest exchange is expanded and earlier work is accessible through history.
- Add a dated syllabus with past, current, and future units; verify the initial lesson follows the current unit. Try "We're on props now", "Skip orientation", "I already know this", and "We haven't started React yet". Check Home and a new thread honor the correction. Try ambiguous/yearless dates and confirm Orbit asks rather than inventing a schedule. Add notes afterward and verify earlier syllabus items remain.
- Review Home, Study, Calendar, Library, and space setup on desktop and mobile. Try a question as a starting point, resume a lesson after navigating away, and check keyboard focus, the menu, and reduced motion. Confirm lesson controls, conversation, applied activities, quizzes, and retries remain usable on a phone.
- Start with no spaces and try the class, curiosity, and material entry points. Add one space, then several (including a long name); verify Home stays compact and the full list remains accessible in navigation and Library. Check the mobile menu, Escape, keyboard focus, and reduced motion.
- Add a PDF to a chosen space and check its material list and any extracted dates in Calendar. Check a failed upload and retry, ambiguous dates, and a Home preview with more than three upcoming items. Leave Study for Home or Calendar and resume; verify the current lesson, conversation, activity, quiz, and mastery remain intact.
- Explain a useful analogy or complete an activity, continue past several conversation turns, then request another explanation, example, activity, or quiz. Check that Orbit can refer naturally to relevant earlier work without repeating it every time or bringing in unrelated topics. Try an incorrect quiz answer and its adapted follow-up; verify mastery and Continue still work. Refresh to check that useful anchors survive without a memory panel or internal labels.
- During Learn, try "Let me try it", submit a response, revise it, and ask about the result in conversation. Check that feedback stays concise, mastery stays unchanged, and a new activity replaces the previous one. Try failed generation and feedback requests, retry without losing your response, and choose "Quiz me" before an activity, after feedback, and while an activity request is pending. Check the response input on mobile and with a keyboard.

Try a failed send and retry, both lesson shortcuts, and "Quiz me" while a reply is pending; confirm the existing quiz, follow-up, and mastery flow still works. Check input usability on mobile and with a keyboard.
- Start a session with and without topic mastery; confirm a short lesson appears before any question. Try "I lost you" and "Give me an example" repeatedly, then "Quiz me"; verify the first question covers the lesson and correct/incorrect answers, follow-ups, and Continue still update the active course's mastery.

- Review the Home background on desktop and narrow screens; check text contrast, mascot visibility, the fade into courses, and the absence of horizontal scrolling.

- Check the Home logo and mascot on desktop and mobile; enable reduced motion and confirm the mascot stays still while the learning action remains easy to reach.
